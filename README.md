## Three quick exercises

### Practice a difficult passage with SpeedPlayer

Open your own practice video or audio recording. Mark A just before the difficult passage and B just after it. Play that segment slowly enough to notice the movement or phrasing. Repeat the same section closer to your target speed before moving the loop to the next passage.

### Explain why one half plus one third is five sixths

Draw two equal-sized bars and split each into six equal parts. Shade three parts in the first bar and two in the second. Put the shaded parts together to show five sixths. Then use FractionSolver to check how both denominators become six before adding. The two bars must represent the same-sized whole.

### Choose PresentCam or PresentCast

Use **PresentCam** when the result should be a video someone can watch later. Start with a short PDF, annotate one point, and record a short explanation. Use **PresentCast** when people are already looking at a shared screen. Put the material in presentation order and check what the audience sees before starting.
